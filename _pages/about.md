Hello! I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the School of Computing and Information Systems (SCIS), [Singapore Management University (SMU)](https://www.smu.edu.sg/), working with [Prof. Guansong Pang](https://sites.google.com/site/gspangsite/) and [Prof. Debin Gao](https://dbgao.github.io/). Prior to joining SMU, I received my PhD degree from [Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT)](https://www.bit.edu.cn/), under the supervision of [Prof. Zhendong Niu](https://pure.bit.edu.cn/zh/persons/zhendong-niu/). My research interests focus on time series forecasting, anomaly detection, foundation models, foundation models for AIOps, and large language models for sequence data <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=1IqAdRwAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN'><img src="https://img.shields.io/endpoint?logo=Google%20Scholar&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.jsdelivr.net%2Fgh%2Fhh4ts%2Fhh4ts.github.io@google-scholar-stats%2Fgs_data_shieldsio.json&labelColor=f6f6f6&color=9cf&style=flat&label=citations"></a>.
